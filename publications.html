Published Journal Articles

Where the network abstains: weak ascent and small CAPE tendency. Schematic.
Bhattarai, A., and Y. Zheng, 2026: Uncertainty-aware machine learning for onset of deep convection: Under what conditions are trigger predictions more reliable?
Geophysical Research Letters, 53(15), e2026GL123315.

Deep convection initiation (DCI) is a longstanding challenge for numerical weather prediction. In this paper, we employ uncertainty-aware deep learning architectures trained on DOE ARM Southern Great Plains (SGP) observations and kilometer-scale hydroclimate reanalysis to identify the specific thermodynamic and kinetic regimes where AI-based trigger predictions can be reliably trusted.

Published Lead Author doi:10.1029/2026GL123315 ↗
WRF places the offshore boundary layer top above the observed one. Schematic.
Wasti, S., Y. Wang, E. Soleimanian, A. Bhattarai, G. Roberts, X. Liu, T. Griggs, and J. Flynn, 2026: Evaluation of WRF-simulated offshore planetary boundary layer height during the TRACER-AQ field campaigns (2021–2023).
Atmospheric Environment, 382, 122250.

Evaluates the offshore planetary boundary layer height (PBLH) simulated by WRF against extensive field campaign measurements (radiosondes, UAVs, and SODAR RASS) during TRACER-AQ over the Gulf of Mexico, with critical implications for regional coastal air quality and ozone dispersion.

Published doi:10.1016/j.atmosenv.2026.122250 ↗
